Splat Express – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-09
Developer: TippyGamesStudio
Contact: tippygamesstudio@gmail.com

Splat Express ("the game", "we") is a casual driving game for Android. This policy explains what information is collected when you play, why, and the choices you have.

Where the game is available: Splat Express is not offered in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If you use it there anyway (for example while travelling), the consent choices described below still apply to you.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. There are no accounts and no chat.
  • Your game progress (coins, upgrades, records, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played, to fix crashes and to adjust game balance. These services collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • If you open the game in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads and before analytics, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings.

Information collected by third-party services

Advertising: Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK automatically collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps, and app launches);
  • diagnostic information (app and SDK performance).

Where required by law, ads are only personalised if you consent. Otherwise Google may show non-personalised or limited ads. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

To understand how the game is played (for example how far players drive, which upgrades they buy, and where they get stuck), the game sends gameplay events together with an app-instance identifier, basic device information (model, OS version, language, country derived from the network) and app usage (sessions, screens, engagement time). We have configured Analytics not to collect the advertising ID and not to use analytics data for advertising. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics only runs if you agree to it ("store and/or access information on a device") in the consent form.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes, a crash report is sent with the stack trace, device information (model, OS version, memory, orientation) and a random installation identifier. We may attach game context such as which world or vehicle you were using.

Remote game tuning: Firebase Remote Config

The game downloads balance settings (such as fuel and coin values). This request includes basic information such as country, language, time zone, OS version and app version.

Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Why we use this information

  • To show ads, which keep the game free, and to measure and limit ad fraud.
  • To fix crashes and improve performance.
  • To improve and balance the game.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We don't use the data collected by the game to identify you personally.

Legal bases (if you use the game in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland)

  • Personalised ads and analytics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, crash reports and game tuning: our legitimate interest in keeping the game free, working and balanced.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Israel, or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we don't know who you are, we may need you to tell us your app-instance ID or other details so we can find data linked to your installation. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We will not treat you differently for using them.

US residents: under some US state laws, showing personalised ads can count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out by turning off ad personalisation, or by deleting your advertising ID in your Android settings. Where offered, you can also opt out in Settings → Privacy settings. The game does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because it is an app and not a website.

International transfers and retention

Google may process this data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. Analytics data is kept for Google Analytics' default retention period. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Advertising data is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): change it any time in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset it or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most devices).
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the game deletes all progress stored on the device.
  • Delete your data: see Delete your data below.

Delete your data

This section explains how to delete data from Splat Express by TippyGamesStudio.

Game progress (coins, upgrades, records, settings) is stored only on your phone. To delete it, uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Splat Express → Storage → Clear storage.

Data held by our service providers. To ask us to delete the analytics and crash data linked to your installation:

  1. Email tippygamesstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Splat Express: delete my data".
  2. Tell us your phone model and roughly when you installed the game, so we can find your installation.
  3. We will reply within 30 days and confirm when the deletion is done.

What is deleted: Google Analytics for Firebase events and Firebase Crashlytics crash reports linked to your installation's random identifiers. We keep no copies.
What is kept: nothing by us. Even without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after Google Analytics' retention period and crash reports after 90 days. We don't hold advertising data. Google controls it under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can break the link to your past ad data at any time by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and its Play Store target audience is 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

All data collected by these services is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
